Archive Workflow

How to Utilize the Archive

Indo Pacific Stock · Rights Managed Motion Picture Archive

1. Search Bar

Use our global search bar to instantly isolate specific clips. Our search engine is optimized for filtering across Geographic Locations (oceans, seas, regions, specific destinations) and Biological Taxonomy (species, genus, family groups, common names, or Latin scientific names). All clips are captioned with metadata tracking specific animals, behaviours, habitats and locations.

To accelerate your search workflow, utilize these protocols:

  • Partial Key Matching: Case-insensitive partial words work efficiently. Inputting “Barra” will instantly isolate all barracuda clips.
  • Spacing Concerns: Input the simplest part of the word and results will show up. For example, searching “fan” will simultaneously show both “seafan” and “sea fan” results.
  • Dynamic Inclusions: Root words will automatically pull relevant sub-taxonomies. Inputting man will isolate manta, manta rays, or “man in the background”.
  • Technical Queries: For precise timeline matching, you can search by camera resolution or codec (Raw or ProRes).

2. Licensing Tiers

Select any visual asset tile to view its Rights-Managed (RM) rates. Content is categorized into Standard or Premium tiers. Per-second billing rates apply for all tiers. A 10-second minimum applies to each clip asset individually — seconds cannot be combined across different clips to meet the minimum. Multiplier rates will apply depending on the usage terms, territories, and duration of use. Bulk rates may apply — please inquire directly.

Editorial Use Only clips (for example footage with visible branding, private property, or people without a model release) cannot be licensed for advertising. Those clips show only Digital Editorial (1.0×) and Documentary Broadcast & SVOD (2.5×). Theatrical, commercial, and exclusivity multipliers are not offered on editorial assets.

3. Direct Inquiry

For a single file, click “Request license” on the clip page. That opens the license inquiry form with the Reel ID already filled in. We then send the complete clip in low-resolution, time-coded format.

If you are after more than one clip, add them to the cart and submit a license request. The form lists every Reel ID so we can send proxies and custom pricing without a follow-up for missing clip names.

4. Submit Timecode Logs

Once you receive your time-stamped low-resolution proxy file, send us the exact “in” and “out” points you wish to license, along with the precise media distribution scope of your project.

5. Invoicing & Payment Terms

Upon receiving your shot logs, we will issue a formal corporate invoice in US Dollars (USD). All licensing fees must be settled via direct wire transfer to our designated corporate bank account in Singapore. Other payment options can be discussed.

6. Master File Delivery

Upon payment validation, final master files are delivered cleanly without visual watermarks. Standard delivery is executed natively in REDCODE® RAW format for camera masters or Apple ProRes for high-resolution aerial drone files. If your post-production timeline requires a custom format, notify us during your timecode submission.
